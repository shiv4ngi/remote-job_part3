02 / The playbook

7 ways to stand out.

More applications ≠ more interviews. A stronger signal beats a longer application count.

01.

Apply for a role, not a keyword.

Read the actual scope. A “GenAI Engineer” might mean model building, agent reliability, or mostly backend APIs. Match the work, not just the title.

02.

Make your first 5 lines count.

Tailor your résumé summary to the job. Mention the stack, your production ownership, and an outcome: latency reduced, cost saved, or quality improved.

03.

Show proof, not certificates.

Link 1–2 relevant projects with a README, architecture, metrics, and trade-offs. A working RAG agent with evaluations beats a dozen course badges.

04.

Use a thoughtful referral.

Reach out to someone on the team with a short, specific note about what you've built and why this particular role matches. Avoid copy-paste asks.

05.

Don't ignore location fine print.

Confirm legal work location, employee vs contractor setup, pay currency, working-hour overlap, and any required office visits before deep interviews.

06.

Prep one production story.

Be ready to explain a real failure: what broke, how you diagnosed it, what you changed, and which metrics proved the fix. That's senior-level signal.

07.

Track and follow up deliberately.

Record your application date, referral, role URL, and next step. One concise follow-up after about a week or two is better than repeated nudges.
